# Crystal Diagnostic Centre — Design System

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Crystal Diagnostic Centre's digital platform is built around a nocturnal luxury aesthetic — combining deep obsidian black (`#000000`) surfaces with crisp typography and precision amber-gold (`#FFC000`) accents. 

Into this dark, high-contrast canvas, white typography and amber-gold highlights are deployed with surgical precision, creating an authoritative, trustworthy, and modern diagnostic interface.

**Key Characteristics:**
- True black (`#000000`) dominant surfaces with white and amber-gold as relief colors
- Neo-Grotesk display typography with tight line-heights and uppercase headers
- Amber-Gold (`#FFC000`) as the signature accent color for primary actions
- Sharp, angular component borders and minimal surface card overlays
- High contrast, accessible patient navigation and appointment tracking

## 2. Color Palette & Roles

### Primary
- **Amber Gold** (`#FFC000`): Signature action accent — used for primary buttons, highlighted indicators, and main callouts.
- **Pure White** (`#FFFFFF`): Primary text color on dark surfaces and header branding.

### Secondary & Accent
- **Dark Gold** (`#917300`): Hover/pressed state for gold action buttons.
- **Gold Text** (`#FFCE3E`): Saturated gold variant used for inline text accents and badges.
- **Cyan Pulse** (`#29ABE2`): Information and status indicator accent.
- **Link Blue** (`#3860BE`): Link hover states across text blocks.

### Surface & Background
- **Absolute Black** (`#000000`): Dominant canvas background.
- **Charcoal** (`#121212`): Secondary surface level for cards and section breaks.
- **Card Surface** (`#181818`): Container backgrounds for test packages and service listings.

## 3. Typography & Hierarchy

- **Display & Section Titles**: `Space Grotesk`, sans-serif — geometric uppercase display face.
- **Body & Controls**: `Inter`, sans-serif — highly readable, crisp body font.
- **Brand Identity**: `Baloo 2` — custom rounded brand lockup.

## 4. Components & Interactive States

- **Primary Action Buttons**: Solid Gold background (`#FFC000`), bold black text (`#000000`), zero border radius, hover transition to `#917300`.
- **Secondary Action Buttons**: Ghost transparent background with subtle white border (`rgba(255,255,255,0.2)`), hover fill `#FFFFFF` with black text.
- **Form Inputs**: Deep charcoal input fields with subtle gold focus rings.
